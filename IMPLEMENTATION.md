## Rename, attachments, privacy and search progress (2026-10-28, continued)

Implemented and verified:
- Folder rename now persists an owner/account-bound operation before IMAP RENAME. Recovery checks the destination UIDVALIDITY after a lost response or restart before completing reference updates. Parent and nested move receipts, encrypted pending/uncertain outbox reply and draft paths, and move-rule destinations are rewritten idempotently. The rename is journaled so other replicas remap their local drafts, pending actions and undo paths. Old-account operations are cancelled rather than mutating the replacement account.
- Preference PATCH updates are serialized with journal writes so changing appearance cannot overwrite a concurrently renamed rule. New scheduled messages remap stale paths only when their source UIDVALIDITY matches the recorded rename generation. Existing send receipts remain idempotent even after their scheduled time passes or while rename recovery is pending. A pre-SMTP rename pause requeues delivery with a retry deadline, retains the encrypted payload, and does not starve other owners. The proxy forwards no browser credentials/referrer, disables environment proxies, validates every redirect, resolves and pins public IP addresses at the actual TCP dial, rejects private/link-local/CGNAT/reserved ranges, enforces deadlines and a 10 MB file/40-megapixel bound, and validates raster signatures.
